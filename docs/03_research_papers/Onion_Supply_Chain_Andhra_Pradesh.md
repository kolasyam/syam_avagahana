# Econometric & Agronomic Research
## Onion Supply Chain Dynamics and Price Volatility in Andhra Pradesh

### 1. Production Clusters & Seasonality

Andhra Pradesh is both a major producer and consumer of onions:
- **Major Production Hub:** Kurnool district accounts for over 60% of Andhra Pradesh's onion cultivation, predominantly in the Kharif and Late Kharif seasons.
- **Secondary Belts:** Anantapur, Kadapa, and parts of Prakasam district.
- **Consumption Centers:** High-density urban demand in Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada-Guntur capital region, and Tirupati.

### 2. Harvest Cycles & Seasonal Vulnerability

| Season | Sowing | Harvest | Market Influx Peak | Storage Vulnerability |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Kharif** | June–July | October–November | Late October | Very High (High moisture, 25-35% post-harvest spoilage) |
| **Late Kharif** | September–October | January–February | February | Moderate |
| **Rabi** | December–January | April–May | May | Low (Excellent storage quality with lower moisture content) |

### 3. The Kharif Transition Window (October Benchmark)

During mid-to-late October, market transitions from stored Rabi stocks (sourced from Maharashtra - Lasalgaon/Nashik) to fresh local Kharif arrivals:
- Weather disruptions or delayed monsoon withdrawal can cause severe transport bottlenecks and high rot in field.
- Wholesale mandi prices in Kurnool fluctuate rapidly between ₹24.00/kg (oversupply) and ₹55.00/kg (delayed harvest).
- Open market retail prices in Vijayawada reach ₹58.00–₹66.00/kg unless government intervention stabilizes supply via Rythu Bazaars.

### 4. Mathematical Price Elasticity of Demand

Empirical econometric studies indicate that household onion demand in urban and semi-urban Andhra Pradesh exhibits an inelastic price elasticity of approximately:

$$\varepsilon_d = -0.32$$

Because consumer demand is highly inelastic, even a minor 10% supply contraction results in a 30% price surge. This confirms the critical necessity of predictive early warning systems like AP-OPIS to deploy strategic buffer stocks ahead of speculative hoarding.
