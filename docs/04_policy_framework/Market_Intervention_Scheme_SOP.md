# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
## Market Intervention Scheme & Price Stabilisation Fund (AP-PSF)

### 1. Trigger Conditions for State Market Intervention

The Government of Andhra Pradesh intervenes in the onion market under two distinct operational mandates:

1. **Farmer Protection (Price Crash Trigger):**
   - **Condition:** Kurnool Mandi modal price falls below ₹18.00/kg (below cost of cultivation).
   - **Action:** AP Markfed initiates minimum support price procurement directly at mandi yards.

2. **Consumer Welfare (Price Spike Trigger):**
   - **Condition:** 14-day forecast indicates retail prices crossing ₹45.00/kg or open mandi exceeding ₹35.00/kg.
   - **Action:** AP Civil Supplies Corporation activates buffer stock liquidation through Rythu Bazaars.

### 2. Aadhaar Biometric Distribution Protocol

To eliminate black-marketing and trader diversion:
- Subsidized onions are rationed at **₹35.00 per kg** (maximum 2 kg per family per week).
- Transactions authenticated via e-PoS (electronic Point of Sale) terminals linked to the civil supplies Aadhaar ration database.
- Real-time sale quantities uploaded automatically to the AP-OPIS data fabric.

### 3. Buffer Stock Release Logistics

- Stock mobilized from NAFED cold storage facilities in Nashik / Kurnool.
- Transport subsidies funded via the Price Stabilisation Fund.
- Target daily distribution volume: 500 to 1,500 MT across high-consumption urban municipal zones.
